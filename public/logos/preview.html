Usage Notes

Wordmark

Use horizontally across the site header and footer. Maintain clear space equal to the height of the ornament on all sides. Do not stretch or recolor.

Badge / Seal

Best for profile images, app icons, stamps on packaging, and social media avatars. Works at all sizes from 32px to full-bleed.

Icon Mark

Use as a standalone symbol where space is limited — favicon, loading screen, embossed watermark. The sparkle star must remain visible.
